How Stripe MRR is derived

MRR comes from active subscription prices normalized to monthly amounts (yearly ÷ 12, weekly × 4.345). Coupons reduce net MRR; start and end months are counted in full; trialing and incomplete subscriptions, and usage-based prices, are skipped. These are approximations — reconcile against finance-owned revenue before treating the numbers as decision-grade. Costs are not imported from Stripe: keep using CSV cost uploads, which remain available in the Data workspace.
